# Verified U.S. Source Benchmarks | NCES School Pulse Panel

**Evidence status:** Agency-published U.S. school-survey statistics, **not** a new analysis performed in this repository. Data collection was **December 2024**; figures are quoted from the official NCES School Pulse Panel published results. Accessed for research planning 25 November 2026.

**Primary source:** https://nces.ed.gov/surveys/spp/results.asp  
**Study program and methodology:** https://nces.ed.gov/surveys/spp/

## Officially published reference estimates

| Published indicator (survey question paraphrased) | Share of U.S. public schools or school-leader responses, as stated by NCES |
|---|---:|
| Public schools reporting a written policy on students' use of AI in school | **31%** |
| School leaders reporting at least some teachers use AI for lesson plans | **55%** |
| Public schools providing AI-use training to some or all teachers | **67%** |
| Public school leaders reporting all students are taught ethical/appropriate AI use | **14%** |

**Interpretation guardrails:** These are separate survey items and may have different response bases/definitions; no individual school-level linkage has been accessed here. The 67% and 14% estimates should **not** be subtracted to infer an actual school-by-school 'AI preparedness gap'. This note is only an authentic U.S. evidence source inventory. No reanalysis of microdata, standard errors, subgroup disparities, causal effects or publication-level novelty has been independently verified.
